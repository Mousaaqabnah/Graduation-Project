Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how MatchField collects, uses, and shares personal information when you use our website and services.

1. Information we collect

  • Account data: such as name, email, phone, date of birth, gender, and city, when you register or update your profile.
  • Usage data: such as pages viewed, device/browser type, and approximate timestamps, to keep the service secure and improve features.
  • Booking data: content you submit related to reservations, reviews, or support requests, as needed to provide those features.

2. How we use information

We use personal information to create and manage accounts, authenticate users, show relevant fields and bookings, send operational notifications, prevent abuse, comply with law, and improve the product.

3. Legal bases (where applicable)

Depending on your region, we may rely on consent (for example, marketing where required), performance of a contract (providing the service you signed up for), legitimate interests (security and analytics, balanced against your rights), or legal obligation.

4. Sharing

We may share information with service providers that help us host the application, send email, or process data under strict confidentiality. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users and the public.

5. Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, including legal, accounting, or reporting requirements, unless a longer period is required by law.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. No online service is completely secure; please use a strong, unique password.

7. Your choices

Where applicable law provides rights (access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection), you may exercise them through in-app settings or by contacting support. You can withdraw marketing consent at any time.

8. International transfers

If your data is processed in countries other than your own, we take steps consistent with applicable law to safeguard it (for example, appropriate contractual clauses).

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will post the new version here and adjust the “Last updated” date. Where required, we will provide additional notice.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact options in the app or your organization’s official support channel.
